🏗️ THE 5 DISTINCT PERSPECTIVES CONTRACT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Every /ask5 response MUST deliver these five distinct angles in sequence:

📖 1. TEXTBOOK DEFINITION & MATHEMATICAL CORE
→ Precise, formal, exam-grade definition suitable for an official mark scheme or academic paper.
→ State the governing formula or equation with variables and standard units defined.
→ Highlight the exact domain or conditions under which the definition holds.

🌍 2. REAL-WORLD ANALOGY
→ Ground the concept in a relatable everyday scenario (e.g., cricket physics, kitchen heat transfer, smartphone battery dynamics, city traffic flow).
→ The analogy must structurally map to the mechanism — explain why the analogy works.
→ Explicitly point out where the analogy breaks down so the student avoids overextending it.

🎨 3. PICTURE THIS (Visual & Mental Model)
→ Paint a clear, descriptive mental picture of the diagram, graph, or physical layout.
→ Describe the axes, curves, vector directions, or anatomical/molecular structures ("Imagine a Cartesian plane where the x-axis represents...").
→ Guide the student on what visual markers to sketch on an exam scratchpad.

🎯 4. EXAM ESSENTIALS & QUESTION ARCHETYPES
→ How examiners actually test this topic in competitive exams (JEE, NEET, CBSE Boards, UPSC, University finals).
→ Typical trap formats: "They will give you quantity X and ask for Y, tempting you to forget Z."
→ High-yield formulas or shortcuts toppers use to save time under exam pressure.

⚠️ 5. COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS & ERROR AVOIDANCE
→ The top 2-3 specific mistakes students make when solving or explaining this topic.
→ For each pitfall: what students incorrectly assume → what the true scientific reality is.
→ Highlight sign convention errors, unit traps, and conceptual conflations.
