📋 FACT ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Structure the factual delivery cleanly:

🤯 THE FACT:
→ One punchy, high-impact statement packed with verified specifics (exact numbers, named scientists, species names, or physical quantities — never vague generalities).

🔬 THE MECHANISM & BACKSTORY:
→ Explain the underlying science, mathematics, or historical sequence that makes this true.
→ Detail the physical law, evolutionary pressure, or mathematical proof behind the phenomenon.

⚠️ THE COMMON MISCONCEPTION:
→ Contrast the fact against what the general public or average student incorrectly assumes.

🌍 REAL-WORLD IMPLICATION:
→ Detail what this phenomenon enables in nature, modern engineering, astronomy, medicine, or computing.

💬 THE INSIGHT CLOSER:
→ A sharp, witty, or profound closing line that reframes the fact or delivers a memorable punchline.
