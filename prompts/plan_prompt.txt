📋 7-DAY ACTIONABLE PLANNING FRAMEWORK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Structure the study plan across realistic daily blocks:

📋 PLAN OVERVIEW:
→ State the Target Exam (e.g., JEE Main, NEET, CBSE Boards, UPSC, College Finals), Student Stage (11th, 12th, Dropper, Undergraduate), and Core Focus Area for the week.
→ State the estimated daily time budget (e.g., 6-8 hours for full-time aspirants; 3-4 hours for school-going students).

📅 DAY-BY-DAY SCHEDULE (Days 1 through 6):
For each day, structure into 3-4 distinct time blocks:
→ 🌅 MORNING BLOCK (Deep Focus, 2-3 hrs): High-difficulty conceptual learning and problem-solving (e.g., Physics Mechanics, Complex Numbers, Organic Reaction Mechanisms) when cognitive energy is highest.
→ ☀️ AFTERNOON BLOCK (Secondary Subject, 2-3 hrs): Interleaved different discipline (e.g., Physical Chemistry, Zoology, Algorithms) to prevent cognitive burnout.
→ 🌇 EVENING BLOCK (Active Practice & PYQs, 1-2 hrs): Solving previous years' questions (PYQs), timed problem sets, or flashcard retrieval practice.
→ 🌙 NIGHT REVIEW (Consolidation, 30-45 min): Formula sheet review, summary consolidation, and setting up next day's desk.

📅 DAY 7 — WEEKLY RESET & CALIBRATION:
→ Full-length or sectional timed mock test under exam conditions.
→ Detailed error analysis (cataloging silly mistakes, conceptual gaps, and time-management flaws).
→ Light revision of the week's top 3 weak areas — no heavy new topics.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⚙️ COGNITIVE SCIENCE PLANNING RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. INTERLEAVING: Never schedule the same subject in back-to-back blocks. Interleaving subjects enhances neural discrimination and retention.
2. BUFFER ZONES: Include 30-45 minute buffer slots across the week. Unforeseen delays happen; realistic plans bend without breaking.
3. SPECIFICITY OVER VAGUENESS: Name exact chapters and subtopics (e.g., "Rotational Motion — Moment of Inertia Theorems & Rolling Dynamics", not just "Physics").
4. SUSTAINABILITY: A plan executed at 85% completion beats an impossible 14-hour grind that leads to burnout by Day 3.
