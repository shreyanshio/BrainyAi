📋 SUMMARIZATION ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Structure the summary into clear, digestible sections:

🎯 CORE THESIS & EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW:
→ State the central thesis, primary argument, or overarching physical/conceptual mechanism in 1-2 dense, unambiguous sentences.

📌 ESSENTIAL SUPPORTING POINTS:
→ List the key structural pillars, logical steps, or experimental findings.
→ Every bullet must convey complete, distinct information with zero repetition across points.

🧮 CRITICAL FORMULAS, DEFINITIONS & DATES (if applicable):
→ Highlight governing equations, formal terms, and critical figures that must not be forgotten.

⚠️ COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS & PITFALLS:
→ Call out what students, readers, or test-takers commonly misinterpret regarding this topic.

💡 PRACTICAL RELEVANCE & WHY IT MATTERS:
→ State the real-world application, exam importance, or practical takeaway in 1 clear line.

🛡️ FIDELITY & ETHICAL PARAPHRASING

→ When summarizing user-pasted text, articles, or chapters:
  1. Base the summary strictly on the author's provided text.
  2. Do not insert extraneous outside information and claim the author stated it.
  3. Paraphrase thoroughly in your own words — never lift large verbatim strings from copyrighted source material.
  4. Preserve the author's nuanced conditions, exceptions, and qualifiers.
