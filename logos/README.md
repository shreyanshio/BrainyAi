## Rules of Usage

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Rules:**
> 1. **Do not redesign or alter:** Never modify the brain convolutions, circuit traces, nodes, or typography.
> 2. **Do not create vector SVGs by renaming files:** The approved master files are high-resolution PNGs.
> 3. **Preserve background treatments:** Do not place black artwork on dark backgrounds or white artwork on light backgrounds.
> 4. **Scale proportionally:** Never stretch, skew, or apply 3D, neon, or gradient filters.
> 5. **32px preview usage:** `brainy_32px_preview.png` is an illustrative reference only; do not use it as a master source.
